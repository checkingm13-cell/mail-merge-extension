🛡️ 24/7 Production Health & Error Troubleshooting Guide

Self-Healing Active

Zero-downtime reliability guidelines, common Google failure modes, and automated recovery actions

1. Sheet Disconnect / "No Recipients" AUTO-HEALED

Cause: Closing compose too fast or Drive session timeout.
Safeguard: Extension now enforces a 1.2s "Saved" state commit buffer on schedule, and waits up to 10s for Google Drive sheet hydration on open.

💡 Manual Fix: Click "↻ Retry Now" while draft is open with sheet attached.
2. Workspace Daily Limit (1,500/day) QUOTA GUARD

Cause: Google Workspace limits multi-send to 1,500 recipients per 24 hours per account.
Safeguard: Extension's Quota Guard auto-pauses campaigns if limit is reached to prevent Google account strikes.

💡 Action: Stagger campaigns across multiple Google accounts (/u/0/, /u/1/).
3. Tab Sleep / Discarding 1-CLICK POLICY

Cause: Chrome Memory Saver sleeps background tabs after 30-60 mins, freezing timers.
Safeguard: Run SETUP_FRESH_PC.bat on any PC to permanently exempt Gmail, Docs, and Drive from tab discard.

💡 Action: Included in extension folder, or click below:
4. Drive Rate Limit ("Can't open sheet") AUTO-RETRY

Cause: Rapid spreadsheet API access triggers Google temporary rate throttle.
Safeguard: Automator detects this dialog, captures visual forensic proof, closes error modal, and auto-retries after 2 minutes.

💡 Action: No manual action needed; auto-recovers after cooldown.
5. Dynamic Links & Deliverability RESOLVED

Cause: Relative links or domain masking triggers spam warnings.
Safeguard: Use {{senderDomain}} in templates. The extension dynamically converts all links into authentic domain links.

💡 Action: Let Gmail handle unsubscribe natively; use real domain links.
6. Session / Context Invalidated 15M KEEP-ALIVE

Cause: Chrome extension reloaded or updated while Gmail tab was open.
Safeguard: Gentle 15-minute background session ping keeps tabs fresh.

💡 Action: Press F5 to refresh any open Gmail tab after reloading extension.

📖 Simple Operator Guide: Running 24/7 Campaigns & Fixing Errors

🚀 Step-by-Step: How to Run Campaigns Every Day

  1. Keep 1 Gmail tab open: Make sure your Gmail tab is open in Chrome. Do not close Chrome or your laptop lid.
  2. Compose & Connect Sheet: Click Compose in Gmail ➡️ Connect your Google Sheet via Mail Merge ➡️ Click the blue "Schedule" button.
  3. Pick Template & Time: Select your template (e.g. IJSR Research Paper Submission) ➡️ choose your send date/time ➡️ click "Schedule & Save Draft".
  4. Let it run automatically: That's it! The extension will wake up, check your audience, and send all emails at the exact scheduled time.

⚠️ What Happens If You Do NOT Run SETUP_FRESH_PC.bat?

Chrome has a battery-saver feature called "Memory Saver" that puts background Gmail tabs to sleep after 30–60 minutes, and Windows automatically puts laptops/PCs to sleep after 15–30 minutes or when the lid is closed.

  • The consequence: When the schedule triggers at 2:00 AM, Gmail and Windows are asleep, so the draft disconnects or throws a "No recipients" error.
  • The 1-Click Fix: Right-click SETUP_FRESH_PC.bat and choose "Run as administrator" once on that PC. It automatically configures Chrome to never sleep Gmail, Docs, or Drive, disables background timer throttling, keeps Chrome active in background, and configures Windows power so the PC never sleeps while plugged in.

🎯 Error Guide: Which Errors Can Be Re-Run vs. Which Cannot?

Error You See Can You Re-Run? What Happened & How to Fix It
📄 Sheet Disconnected / "No Recipients" ✅ YES (1-Click) Draft was closed before Google Drive finished saving rows. Open the draft in Gmail, re-attach the Google Sheet, then click "↻ Retry Now" in the Dashboard.
🌐 Internet / Wi-Fi Disconnected ✅ YES (1-Click) Wi-Fi dropped during send. Reconnect your internet and click "↻ Retry Now".
🔄 Drive Rate Limit ("Can't open sheet") ✅ YES (Auto) Google temporarily throttled sheet access. The extension auto-retries in 2 minutes, or click "↻ Retry Now".
🔑 Login Expired ("Verify it's you") ✅ YES Google session timed out. Open Gmail, type your password to sign back in, then click "↻ Retry Now".
🗂️ Draft Missing / Subject Mismatch ❌ NO AUTO-RETRY The email draft was manually deleted or sent from Drafts. You must create a new draft from your template and schedule it.
⏳ Daily Limit Reached (1,500/day) ⏳ WAIT 12-24H Google's Workspace limit (1,500 emails in 24 hours) is full. The extension pauses sending until Google resets the limit tomorrow, or you can switch to another sender account.

📝 How to Customize Links and Slugs Without Breaking Anything

You have full freedom to change any link in the Templates Manager tab:

  • Use {{senderDomain}} in your links (e.g. https://{{senderDomain}}/upload-article). The extension automatically replaces it with your active sender domain.
  • Click "<> HTML Source" in the template editor if you want to paste full HTML code from your journal website.
